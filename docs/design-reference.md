# The Animus Archive — design reference

Findings from the reference-image review (six mockups: homepage, characters directory,
Ezio hero close-up, full Ezio profile, relationships page, full Edward profile + hero close-up).
The mockups are **design references only**. Their text, dates, quotes and relationship labels
were generated by an image model and are not a source of truth.

## Identity

- Wordmark: "The Animus Archive" with an original mark. No Ubisoft logo or official insignia.
- Fan-project disclaimer in the footer.
- Navigation: **Home / Characters / Relationships** + search + bookmarks. The mockups also
  show Timeline, Factions, Locations, Lore, Artifacts, Compare — all deliberately excluded.
- Hamburger only on narrow screens.

## Type

| Role | Face | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Cinzel | Headlines, names, section titles, spaced-caps labels |
| Body | EB Garamond | Biography, editorial copy, info-table values, italic lines |
| UI | Inter | Small controls, filters, search results, dense metadata |

No script/handwriting face. "Requiescat in pace" etc. is set in italic Garamond.

## Colour

- Near-black warm base, aged parchment for editorial panels. Profiles alternate dark and light.
- **Crimson is reserved** for active / selected / one key word per title. Never on every card.
- Each character has a **secondary accent** drawn from their setting (Ezio: Renaissance gold,
  Edward: weathered bronze + muted turquoise, Bayek: desert gold …) used for rules, map lines
  and small ornaments.
- Most colour comes from the artwork, not from UI chrome.

## Recurring patterns

- **Title block**: spaced eyebrow → large caps title with one crimson word → diamond divider →
  spaced-caps subtitle.
- **Section heading**: short crimson bar at left + large spaced serif title.
- **Buttons**: crimson outline + arrow; no fills, no glow.
- **Selected state (site-wide)**: crimson wash over art + thin crimson border + diamond marker.
- **Active nav item**: a hairline that flares out from a hollow diamond — dark core, white-hot ring, red bloom — tapering to nothing at both ends (`.nav-active` in globals.css).
- **Parchment panel**: torn left edge that dissolves into the art, paper grain.
- **Engravings**: one small map + one architectural sketch per character. Never more than one
  decorative set per panel.
- **Emblem**: per character (Florence lily, Jolly Roger…). Card insignia reflects real
  affiliation (Assassin / Hidden One, Templar, independent) — Kassandra and Eivor are not
  Assassins; Haytham and Shay are Templars.

## Home

**Binding reference (added 2026-10-24): the Edward-selected homepage mockup.** The user asked
for the homepage to follow it closely. What it specifies:

- Whole hero, featured strip included, fits the first screen (1440×900).
- Left column: spaced eyebrow "Explore · Discover · Connect", three-line headline at a moderate
  size (~4.4rem, not a giant display), two-line intro, crimson outline CTA with a soft glow.
- Scene behind everything; character in the centre-left, landmark in the right half; strong
  dark vignette on the left, darker right edge behind the right column.
- Right column: italic quote + short crimson rule + name, then a vertical marker list (setting
  first with a glowing crimson ring, middle dots, hollow end ring).
- Strip header: crimson bar, spaced title, hairline rule, "View all characters →" at right.
- Five landscape 3:2 cards across, each a bust in its own setting with a dark lower half;
  centred faction mark, name, dates. Selected: crimson border + crimson glow. Round arrows with
  crimson outlines outside the row.
- Deliberate deviations: the mockup's quote "A man chooses. A slave obeys." is Andrew Ryan's
  line from BioShock, not Edward's, so only verified quotes appear (others show the editorial
  line without quotation marks); the strip says "Featured characters" because Kassandra and
  Eivor are not Assassins; dates come from the verified data.

Earlier notes from Reference 1:

- Hero: headline stacked left ("PEOPLE / SHAPE / **HISTORY**"), character at ~30–60 % width
  facing right, landmark in right half, verified quote or unattributed editorial line at right.
- One data-driven hero. Selecting a featured character swaps art, palette, metadata, quote and

## Characters directory

- Short banner → **era timeline** (thin rule + diamond nodes, the primary filter) → filter
  sidebar + grid.
- Cards are tall portraits **set in the character's own environment** (that is what keeps the
  grid from looking uniform), ~2:3, 5–6 per row at 1440 px.
- Filters: role (protagonist / playable / modern day), affiliation, game, favourites. Mentors /
  Allies / Rivals are relationship types, not character types — they live on Relationships.
- Sort: chronological / name / release.
- Only one era active at a time; active filters show as removable chips with "Clear all".
- Mockup errors not carried over: "Lasmond Miles", "Ayo", nameless cards, Classical-era tile
  with no characters, most of the printed lifespans.

## Profile (Ezio is the benchmark, Edward confirms the template)

1. **Hero** — art ~58 % / parchment dossier ~42 %, torn edge. Left margin in the hero:
   epigraph, emblem, theme words. After the hero the left rail becomes the sticky section
   nav (scroll-spy, crimson dot). The dossier panel follows its own reference, below.
2. **Biography** — three-part band on the archive surface (see below).
3. **Key moments** — horizontal rail of plates with crimson markers on a hairline.
4. **Relationships** — shared map component (see below).
5. **Gallery** — horizontal strip of plates + lightbox; only real assets.
6. **Legacy** — wide closing scene, short summary, parting quote, link back, archive branding.
   Every profile gets one: a dossier closes on its own parting line, everyone else on their
   own quote, the way the reference repeats a character's signature at the end.

Natural document flow. Nothing is scaled to fit the viewport.

**No family tree.** It was a second drawing of the people the Relationships map already
shows, so it was removed (2026-10-25) along with `lib/family.ts` and its component.

### Archive surfaces

**Binding reference (added 2026-10-25): the full-page and per-section close-ups.** After the
hero, every section runs dark, and none of them is a flat fill:

- `.archive` — charcoal pooled at the top-left and in the character's own `--sky` at the
  bottom-right, a scored top and bottom edge, and `scratches.svg` over the existing grain.
- `.plate` — scored border, black bevel and inner shadow: the frame for every thumbnail.
- `.seam` — hairline between blocks that rusts crimson towards its middle.
- `.accent-bar` — the section heading's crimson bar, with the reference's bloom.
- `.etched` — a photograph read as an engraving (grayscale, high contrast, `screen`), used at
  5–6 % opacity for the architectural line art. It is the character's **own** setting art, so
  the engraving behind each profile is that character's city and nothing is invented.
- `feather-edges` / `feather-x` / `feather-y` — artwork dissolves into the page instead of
  ending on a rectangle. Two linear masks intersected: one radial cannot do it, because sized
  to clear the corners it leaves the sides opaque and sized to clear the sides it eats the
  picture.

### Biography band

Left: heading, the opening two paragraphs, and a crimson outline button that unfolds the rest
in place. Centre: the character in their world (the shared `Scene`), feathered into the page.
Right: an editorial pull-line — never dressed as dialogue, since the character's own quotes
belong to the hero and the Legacy close — then attribution, themes and aliases, over the
engraving of their city.

### Key moments, Gallery and Biography plates

Each directory character has official art of their own, found on the wiki and listed in
`scripts/art/sources.json` (see `docs/assets.md` for how it is chosen): one image for the
biography band, five for the gallery strip, and a pool of eight cycled over the key moments.

There is still no artwork **of the events themselves**, and inventing some would be a picture
of something that never happened — so a moment's plate is related art from that character's
game, not an illustration of that moment. Plates are graded at build time towards a common
luminance, because concept art is often drawn on white and glares against the near-black
sections. Moment thumbnails are cropped to 5:3 on the most detailed part of the picture, since
a figure drawn to one side of an empty canvas centre-crops badly.

Where a character has no plates, the rail falls back to stepping a window across the panorama
of their setting, and the gallery to their two composed busts plus crops of that panorama.

### Key moments data

`timeline` lives on `Character`, not on `Dossier`: a character can have a checked timeline long
before anyone writes their full biography, and all twenty directory characters have one. Dates
were checked against each character's AC-wiki page. Where a date is genuinely uncertain the
event is left out rather than guessed, which is why some timelines are shorter than others.

### Hero dossier panel

**Binding reference (added 2026-10-25): the Ezio hero-panel close-up.** Top to bottom:

- Spaced game titles → short crimson bar.
- **One line** of large Cinzel caps for the name — no emblem beside it. The size is computed
  from measured Cinzel advances (`lib/typeset.ts`) against the column read in `cqw`, so every
  name from "Alexios" to "Ratonhnhaké:ton" fills the column on a single line instead of
  wrapping. Capped at 54 px so short names do not dwarf the page.
- Crimson spaced epithet (or setting) with a hairline rule running to the column's edge.
- Italic quote, then a short crimson rule + spaced-caps attribution. Characters with no
  verified line get the editorial summary, unquoted.
- Thin divider, then **two columns**: small spaced-caps labels / serif values at the left, the
  cartouche at the right. The row naming an allegiance carries a small crimson faction mark.
- **Cartouche** (`components/profile/Cartouche.tsx`): a framed map fragment with the character's
  place pinned in crimson, the engraved compass in the opposite corner, coordinates beneath,
  and the signature line + attribution under that. The linework is a graticule and open
  contours — deliberately generic, because a traced coastline would claim to be a real map of
  somewhere. Coordinates and signature appear only where they are recorded; everyone else gets
  the fragment pinned with their birthplace. Hidden below `sm`, where it would crowd the page.
- Paper vignette over the whole sheet, in addition to the existing grain.

The panel is a grid column rather than an absolutely-positioned block, so a long dossier makes
the hero taller instead of being clipped top and bottom.

Deliberate deviation: the reference's handwritten "Requiescat in pace." is set in italic
Garamond like every other quoted line, and is only shown for characters who actually have such
a line (`dossier.signature`).

## Relationship map (profile + Relationships page share one component)

- Fixed slot layout, not force-directed: Family (top), Mentors & legacy (left), Allies (right),
  Love & marriage (lower left), Rivals & enemies (lower right), Next generation (bottom).
- Types are distinguished by **line style + muted palette-friendly hues**, not six saturated
  colours. Group tags label the boxes; no duplicate labels on the lines.
- Legend doubles as a type filter.
- Relationships page: node click re-centres; profile: node click opens that profile.
- Narrow screens: stacked group lists, not a squeezed graph.
- One person may hold several relationships (Mario: uncle + mentor).

## Hero art spec (asked from the user per character)

- 16:9, ≥ 2560×1440, no text/UI.
- Character ~30–60 % from left, knees-up, head in upper third, turned/stepping right.
- Left ~30 % environment, darker (homepage headline). Right ~40 % calmer (parchment / quote).
- Landmark in the right half. Consistent golden-hour-style lighting.
- Separate 2:3 bust portrait (≥ 800×1200) for cards, search and map nodes.
